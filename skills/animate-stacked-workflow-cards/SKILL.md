# Stacked workflow cards

Keep controls in stable document order. Promote the selected card visually while preserving reading order. Let a tap persist selection and collapse the stack when space is limited.

Open the [browser example](examples/example.html) and inspect the accompanying [JavaScript](examples/example.js). Use it as a starting point for the interaction, structure, and fallback states.

## Verify

Select each card with the keyboard and check that all three cards remain accessible on mobile.
