# Monitoring app landing pages

Lead with what is monitored and what an operator can act on. Pair a dashboard preview with explained states. Avoid relying on color alone.

Open the [browser example](examples/example.html) and inspect the accompanying [JavaScript](examples/example.js). Use it as a starting point for the interaction, structure, and fallback states.

## Verify

Switch status and verify the text describes the state independently of color.
