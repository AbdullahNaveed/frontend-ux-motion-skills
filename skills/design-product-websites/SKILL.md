# Product Website Design

Open the [runnable example](examples/example.html) and inspect its [script](examples/example.js) to see the baseline behavior.

1. Inspect the brief, existing site or repo, primary audience, and conversion path. Identify what a first-time visitor must understand before taking that action. Keep existing routes and working forms unless the brief changes them.
2. Outline the page using the smallest sequence that answers visitor questions. Give each section one job. Pair each major claim with a real screenshot, working preview, workflow, or substantiated result.
3. Choose a showcase based on comparison needs. Use a dark product-card grid for sibling products with distinct destinations. Use a lighter feature grid for capabilities under one product. Use an ordered story when features make sense as a sequence.
4. Define states and action ownership before styling. Use links for navigation and buttons for local actions. A preview can enhance a card, while the title, summary, and action remain available at rest. Keep store badges and secondary links independently reachable.
5. Build the content in semantic source order. Verify the narrow layout and keyboard path before adding motion. Open the primary CTA and confirm loading, success, and recovery where forms are in scope.
6. Verify the page against a real visitor scenario. Check whether the hero communicates the offer, whether the showcase makes choices legible, and whether the CTA arrives at its stated destination.

Use the [website blueprints](references/website-blueprints.md) to choose a section sequence or showcase.
