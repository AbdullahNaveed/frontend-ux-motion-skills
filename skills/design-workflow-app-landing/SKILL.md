# Workflow app landing pages

Lead with the job and a concrete outcome. Order screenshots by task sequence. Repeat the primary action after the walkthrough.

Open the [browser example](examples/example.html) and inspect the accompanying [JavaScript](examples/example.js). Use it as a starting point for the interaction, structure, and fallback states.

## Verify

Open a screen with the keyboard and confirm the preview and selected state agree.
